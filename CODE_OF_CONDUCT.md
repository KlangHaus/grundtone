# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a straightforward, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a good project:

- Being kind and patient with people, and precise about code.
- Accepting correction gracefully — including when the person correcting you is new, or junior, or
  wrong in a way you have to explain.
- Saying what you measured rather than what you assume, and saying plainly when you did not measure
  it.
- Giving credit.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages — and
when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **mail@allanasp.dk**. Reports are handled confidentially, and we
will acknowledge them within 5 working days.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this
Code of Conduct, and will say why when they do.
